## Big finding — read this first

UK has **rich, well-formed search volume** on this topic — opposite of RO. Autocomplete returns 5-10 strong variants per seed. Several insights:

1. **"FSA" alone is ambiguous in EN.** `FSA inspection` returns USDA Farm Service Agency results mixed with UK Food Standards Agency. Solution: never use bare "FSA" as a primary keyword. Use **"food hygiene rating"** or **"food standards agency"** instead.
2. **The numeric rating drives search.** `food hygiene rating 5` has tons of variants (sticker, image, sign, png, checklist) — people care intensely about the rating because it's posted on the door of every UK food business.
3. **"How to improve food hygiene rating"** is the gold-standard search — explicit informational/commercial bridge query. Volume is solid, intent is clear, and Sopia maps directly.
4. **Brand-specific cafe rating searches dominate** (`cafe pacifico food hygiene rating`, `cafe masala food hygiene rating` etc.) — these are individual customers checking ratings, not our target.

## Notes

- **Volume is much higher than RO.** UK food hygiene is a national obsession (the rating is on the door, public on scores.food.gov.uk, customers check before booking).
- **gov.uk is unbeatable for navigational queries** (`food hygiene rating check`). Skip those — focus on commercial-investigation and how-to.
- **Avoid "FSA" alone** — too ambiguous in EN. Always pair with "food" or use "Food Standards Agency" written out at first mention.
- **Cross-promote opportunity**: `food safety checklist for restaurants` ranks in both UK and US — write the UK version first (less saturated), then port for US later.
- **Sopia angle**: lead with the digital-procedures hook ("instead of paper logs that the inspector knows you backfilled, every step has a timestamp"). UK auditors are well-known for spotting backfilled paper logs — this is a real pain point.
